Colorify Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-08

Colorify is a local desktop application. It is designed to adjust display color
settings on your device.

Data Colorify stores locally:
- saved color profiles;
- the currently selected profile and current slider values;
- behavior settings such as auto apply, start with Windows, and start minimized;
- optional wallpaper path if you choose a background image.

Colorify does not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal information.
Colorify does not require an account. Colorify does not include analytics,
advertising trackers, or telemetry in this build.

If you import or export profile files, those files are handled locally on your
device. If you choose a wallpaper image, the selected file path is stored locally
so the app can reload it.

Third-party components used by Colorify may be subject to their own licenses and
notices. See THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt.
